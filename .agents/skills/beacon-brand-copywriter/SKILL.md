# Beacon Brand Copywriter Skill

This skill guides the creation of marketing copy, UI microcopy, and promotional material for Beacon.

## Core Directives

1. **Focus on Tactile Hardware Reality**:
   - Talk about the camera notch, keyboard shortcuts (`⌘⇧B`), the solid obsidian chassis, and instantaneous response times.
   - Contrast Beacon against bloated cloud-based SaaS apps.
2. **The Voice Formula**:
   - **Clarity > Cleverness**: State the benefit directly before embellishing.
   - **Technical Grounding**: Pair emotional benefits with concrete specs (e.g. "Zero battery drain" -> "0.1% CPU, throttles to 0 FPS when collapsed").
   - **Anti-Subscription Philosophy**: Celebrate lifetime ownership ("Buy once, own forever").

## Copy Templates & Formulas

### Hero Headline Formulas
- *Action + Hardware*: "Turn Your MacBook Notch Into an Intentional Living Companion."
- *Contrast*: "Your Mac Has an Unused Hardware Feature. We Made It Your Most Powerful Focus Tool."
- *Anti-SaaS*: "Stop Multitasking in Chaos. Deep Work Lives in Your Notch."

### Feature Callout Formula
- **Headline**: 3–5 words, title case, punchy.
- **Spec / Tag**: 1-2 words (e.g., `0.1% CPU`, `SQLite WAL`, `14-Day Guarantee`).
- **Body**: Max 2 sentences. First sentence states what it does; second sentence states why competitors fail at it.

### Prohibited Words & AI Slop
Do NOT use: *game-changer, synergy, seamless, unlock your potential, revolutionize, all-in-one productivity suite, robust solution*.
