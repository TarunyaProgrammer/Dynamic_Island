# Beacon Growth Marketer Skill

This skill provides battle-tested launch playbooks and distribution strategies for Beacon on macOS.

## Launch Channels & Angles

### 1. Hacker News ("Show HN") Playbook
- **Title Formula**: `Show HN: Beacon – We turned the MacBook notch into an offline habit HUD`
- **Hook**: HN audience loves lightweight, native, offline-first software that respects privacy and rejects SaaS subscription models.
- **Key Talking Points**:
  - Built with local SQLite WAL persistence (no mandatory cloud/telemetry).
  - 0.1% CPU idle footprint and 0 FPS throttling.
  - Multi-tier Gemini failover cascade with sub-second response times (~930ms).
  - One-time purchase ($29) vs recurring monthly rent.

### 2. Twitter / X Viral Demo Video Playbook
- **Visual Hook (0–3s)**: Screen recording of a mouse hovering over the MacBook notch. The notch smoothly expands into the solid obsidian HUD with Solar Amber highlights.
- **Narrative Arc (4–15s)**:
  - "The MacBook notch has been sitting idle on your screen for 3 years."
  - "We built Beacon to turn that dead hardware space into a living focus HUD."
- **Feature Showcase (16–35s)**:
  - Show quick-incrementing a habit (`+10 problems`).
  - Show the 25m Focus Sprint timer running in Ice Cyan.
  - Show summoning the Raycast-style command engine with `⌘⇧B`.
  - Show the Beacon Spirit pet answering a natural language command.
- **CTA (36–45s)**:
  - "Download the free DMG or grab a Pioneer Lifetime License today at beacon.tarunya.me."

### 3. Product Hunt Launch Formula
- **Tagline**: The Intentional Dynamic Island & AI Companion for macOS
- **Makers Comment**:
  - Why we built Beacon (hatred of subscription bloat and context switching).
  - The 6 Behavioral Paradigms (Habit, Accumulative, Deadline, Milestone, Duration, Avoidance).
  - Special launch offer ($29 Lifetime Pioneer pass for the first 500 builders).
